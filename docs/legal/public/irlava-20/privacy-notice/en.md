## 6. Purposes and Legal Bases

### 6.1. Residential Property Management

Personal data are processed to support residential property management, water-consumption accounting, technical meter records and information forming part of the property-management file.

The primary legal basis is **Article 6(1)(c) GDPR** — compliance with a legal obligation applicable to the Controller.

### 6.2. User Accounts and Secure Access

Data are processed to create and maintain user accounts, assign roles and access rights, authenticate users and prevent unauthorised access.

The primary legal basis is **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** — the Controller’s legitimate interest in providing secure digital access.

### 6.3. Account Access Recovery

Data are processed to restore user access securely.

The legal basis is **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR**.

### 6.4. System Security and Audit

Data are processed to prevent misuse, detect unauthorised access, audit material administrative actions and investigate incidents.

The legal basis is **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR**.

### 6.5. Announcements and Communications

Data may be used to deliver residential property management information and operational communications.

Depending on the particular communication, the legal basis may be:

- **Article 6(1)(c) GDPR**, where the communication is required to comply with a legal obligation; or
- **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR**, where the Controller has a legitimate interest in providing effective service communications.

### 6.6. Push Notifications

Where a user enables push notifications through their device or browser, technical information required for delivery is processed.

Push functionality is optional and may be disabled through device or browser settings.

### 6.7. Backups and Service Continuity

Personal data may be included in backups to ensure data availability, system recovery and service continuity.

Data within backups retain the legal basis applicable to the underlying processing. Additional security and continuity processing is based on **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR**.

## 7. Data Minimisation and Protection

{{controller_name}} and the MVX technical service provider give particular attention to the protection of personal data in accordance with the GDPR and applicable Latvian and European Union requirements.

MVX applies the principles of **data minimisation**, **privacy by design** and **privacy by default**.

Only data necessary to provide the relevant function and residential property management process are processed.

Where reasonably and technically practicable, operational data are separated from directly identifying personal information.

Direct identifiers such as first name, surname, e-mail address and telephone number are stored in encrypted form.

The protection model is designed so that, if stored database contents were obtained without authorisation, directly identifying information could not be read without the corresponding cryptographic means and access information.

Access to personal data is provided only to authorised persons according to their role and need.

Technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal data against:

- unauthorised access;
- unauthorised disclosure;
- unauthorised or accidental alteration;
- accidental loss;
- destruction;
- other risks of unauthorised processing.

Service-continuity, backup and controlled-recovery mechanisms are also maintained.

Detailed MVX security and cryptographic architecture is not publicly disclosed where publication could increase system security risk.

## 8. Recipients of Personal Data

Personal data may be accessible to:

- persons authorised by {{controller_name}} according to their duties;
- the MVX technical service provider to the extent necessary to maintain the system;
- external technical-infrastructure providers where necessary for MVX operation;
- other persons or authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Personal data are not sold for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 9. Processing Outside the EEA

Due to the infrastructure of certain external technical service providers, some technical information or personal data may be processed outside the European Economic Area.

Where particular processing qualifies as a transfer of personal data to a third country, the requirements of Chapter V GDPR and the relevant transfer mechanism applicable to the service provider are used.

Further information concerning transfers applicable to particular processing may be requested from the Controller.

## 10. Retention

Personal data are retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, compliance with a legal obligation, security or defence of legal rights.

Retention periods vary according to the category of data.

For example:

- user-account data are retained while access is required;
- security and PII-access audit records are generally retained for up to 24 months;
- rate-limit records are generally retained for up to 7 days;
- inactive push subscriptions and delivery history are generally retained for up to 180 days;
- backups are retained on a rotating basis, with the V0.0 pilot targeting approximately the latest 13 successful weekly copies.

Information required for the property-management file, water accounting, meter technical history or other legal obligations is retained for as long as the relevant management or legal requirement applies.

## 11. Data-Subject Rights

Subject to the GDPR and applicable law, a data subject may have the right to:

- receive information about the processing of their personal data;
- request access to personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- where applicable, request deletion;
- where applicable, request restriction of processing;
- where applicable, object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- exercise other rights provided by the GDPR where the applicable conditions are met.

These rights are not absolute and may be limited where retention or processing is required for compliance with a legal obligation, security, defence of legal rights or another lawful basis.

## 12. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If a data subject considers that personal-data processing does not comply with applicable requirements, they may first contact {{controller_name}}.

The data subject also has the right to lodge a complaint with the Latvian data-protection supervisory authority — **Datu valsts inspekcija**.
