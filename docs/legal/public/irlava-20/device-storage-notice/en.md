# DEVICE STORAGE AND COOKIE NOTICE

**MVX System — {{controller_name}}**

*This document is a translation of the Latvian master version. In the event of any discrepancy, the Latvian version shall prevail.*

## 1. Purpose of this Notice

This Notice explains how MVX uses technical data-storage mechanisms on the user’s device and in the browser.

MVX may use:

- cookies where technically necessary;
- `localStorage`;
- `sessionStorage`;
- PWA Service Worker cache;
- other browser-provided technical storage required for MVX operation.

MVX V0.0 is not intended to use advertising, marketing or behavioural-profiling cookies.

## 2. Technically Necessary Storage

MVX may store information on the device or in the browser where necessary for:

- user authentication and session management;
- retention of user-selected settings;
- language and interface preferences;
- PWA operation;
- Service Worker caching;
- secure system operation;
- push-notification functionality where enabled by the user.

Such storage is used only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant MVX function.

## 3. Cookies

MVX V0.0 is not intended to use:

- advertising cookies;
- marketing cookies;
- behavioural analytics cookies;
- third-party advertising profiling.

Where cookies are technically necessary for authentication, security or another function requested by the user, they may be used without separate consent to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Accordingly, MVX V0.0 does not require a general “Accept all cookies” mechanism where no consent-requiring technologies are used.

## 4. Local Storage and Session Storage

MVX may use browser `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` to retain technical information required for system operation.

This may include, for example:

- user-interface settings;
- language selection;
- technical information required for session or access functionality;
- temporary application state.

These mechanisms are not used for advertising profiling.

## 5. PWA and Service Worker

MVX may operate as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

The PWA Service Worker may cache technical application resources on the device in order to:

- improve loading speed;
- improve operational stability;
- provide PWA technical functionality;
- manage updates of software resources.

The cache is not intended for behavioural profiling.

## 6. Push Notifications

Push notifications are an optional feature.

They are used only where the user has allowed notifications through their device or browser.

Technical information may be retained to provide this feature, including:

- push endpoint;
- required cryptographic keys;
- language or technical settings;
- delivery-status information.

The user may withdraw push-notification permission through device or browser settings.

## 7. External Technical Resources

Certain MVX functions may use external technical resources or services.

When such a resource is requested, the service provider may receive ordinary technical information such as:

- IP address;
- request time;
- browser or device technical information;
- other standard network metadata.

MVX does not use such technical interactions to sell personal data or for advertising profiling.

## 8. Retention

The retention period for technical information depends on the particular mechanism.

Information may be removed:

- at the end of a session;
- after a defined technical period;
- when the user signs out;
- when the user clears browser or application data;
- when the relevant technical record is no longer necessary.

PWA cache may remain on the device until updated, deleted or cleared through browser/PWA data controls.

## 9. User Control

Users may use browser or device settings to:

- delete cookies and locally stored data;
- clear PWA cache;
- withdraw push-notification permission;
- remove the installed PWA.

Deleting technically necessary information may:

- sign the user out of MVX;
- reset user settings;
- temporarily affect system functionality.

## 10. Consent and Future Changes

If MVX later introduces a technology that requires user consent under applicable law, that technology will not be activated before an appropriate user choice is provided.

This Notice will be updated accordingly.
